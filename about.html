About MIS Solutions

Modern advisory rooted in decades of infrastructure experience.

MIS Solutions has supported organizations since 1999, helping teams navigate infrastructure, continuity, virtualization, storage, backup, replication, and cloud change. That background shapes a practical advisory approach for the AWS, Azure, and Microsoft decisions organizations face today.

What carries forward

Experience matters most when a “new” decision sits on top of older complexity.

Many modernization efforts are constrained by systems, processes, and support realities that were built over years. MIS helps organizations account for those conditions instead of pretending every project starts clean.
